What changed

Per-op Python and ATen dispatch largely disappears. Linear, RoPE, residual, and KV-write ranges move inside the captured graph.
Static-buffer staging is now dominant. Tensor copies account for 2.60 ms of the 3.12 ms graph-on request-forward cost.
Sampling remains outside the graph. Sort, top-k, softmax, and multinomial still execute once per decode batch.

Method and limitations
  • Source traces: server_engine_trace_graph_off.json and server_engine_trace_graph_on.json.
  • The dominant clean groups contain six graph-off B=4 decode steps and six graph-on B=8 decode steps.
  • Request-forward time is decode-batch wall time divided by active batch size.
  • Throughput is active batch size divided by mean decode-step wall time.
  • CPU operation time is summed inside the selected engine.decode_batch spans and divided by request-forwards.
  • ATen ranges are nested. Operation rows explain where time appears but must not be summed into the total row.
  • Use benchmarks/bench_engine_cudagraph.py for a controlled same-batch benchmark.
